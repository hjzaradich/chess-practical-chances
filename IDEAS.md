# Ideas (not in spec, not being built)

- **Relative draw rate.** Maia's D is the probability the game ends drawn, so with lots of material at club level it is low almost regardless of how sharp the position is (Berlin endgame D = 0.05 vs Najdorf 0.017 at 1500). If Phase 2 shows raw D has too little resolution, show D relative to a baseline for the same material and ratings. Source: Phase 0, spike 3.
- **Running total of T along the game.** T only covers the next k moves, so a long, hard defence (R+B vs R at club level) shows as many moderate values, never one high one. A cumulative line, or a sum over a selected stretch of plies, would make that visible. Display-only, no new math. Source: Phase 2 discussion (DECISIONS 2.9).
